NOTES: Wiring risks. (1) H1 emphasis: page.jsx splits CORE_SENTENCES[0] on the literal string 'education research'; if the client edits sentence 1 and the phrase disappears, LEAD_AFTER is undefined and the italic phrase still renders appended — the admin needs an emphasis-range or rich-text field for the headline, not a plain string plus a hardcoded split. (3) MAP_COUNTRIES.id keys world-atlas numeric ISO codes on purpose (name drift: Türkiye, Eswatini); the admin country picker must store the numeric code, and a bad id silently drops the country from the map. (4) GlobeBackdrop hardcodes London/Lusaka coords in [lat,lon] while OFFICES stores [lon,lat] — same data, flipped axes, two places to update on an office move; derive globe markers from OFFICES. (5) The '{n} countries shown' figure and the WorldMap svg title are computed from MAP_COUNTRIES.length — no separate count field. (6) TRACK_RECORD figures (15/150/50) are restated verbatim inside PARTNERS_INTRO prose and in SITE.description/home metadata description; a client editing the numbers in settings will not update the prose — either template the prose or flag the duplication in the admin. (7) FooterSignup's ROUTES_WITH_OWN_SIGNUP must stay in sync with which pages embed their own MailingListForm. (8) PartnerLogoWall splits PARTNERS into rows of slice(0,9)/slice(9) — a list that grows past 18 unbalances the two marquee bands; compute the split. (9) SIGNPOSTS[].kicker exists in data but SignpostTrio never renders it — dead field, drop or wire before modelling. (10) Placeholder() sentinel strings (signpost summaries, MAP_SUMMARY) are awaiting client copy; the Placeholder chip auto-disappears once real text replaces them.
